    Read aloud

    Tap the headphones at the top to hear the book from the page you're on. While it reads in Reflowed text, tap any sentence to jump there.

    For the most natural sound, download a Premium or Enhanced voice on your iPhone or iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. Then reopen Folio and pick it here.

    Saved only in this browser on this device, never in your GitHub repo. Costs about US$0.015 per minute of listening (roughly $1 an hour), paid to OpenAI. Set a monthly spending limit in your OpenAI account.
